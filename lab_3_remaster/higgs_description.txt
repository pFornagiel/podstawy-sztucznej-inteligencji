HIGGS

Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/280/higgs
Subset used here: OpenML dataset 23512 (https://www.openml.org/d/23512), 98 050 rows of the original 11 million
Authors: Daniel Whiteson, Pierre Baldi, Peter Sadowski
Introductory paper: P. Baldi, P. Sadowski, D. Whiteson, "Searching for exotic particles in high-energy physics with
deep learning", Nature Communications 5, 4308 (2014), https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.4735
License: CC BY 4.0

Monte Carlo simulations of proton-proton collisions in a particle accelerator. The task is to distinguish a signal
process, in which new Higgs bosons are produced, from a background process with the same particles in the final
state. Every collision ends with a lepton (electron or muon), four jets and missing energy (from neutrinos, which
the detector does not see). The authors normalized the features, so momenta and masses have values around 1.

This file contains a random sample of 60 000 rows (random_state=0) of the OpenML subset, without the single row
with missing values. Values are rounded to 4 significant digits.
60000 rows, 29 columns. The target variable is class (1 - signal, 0 - background).

Columns:

21 low-level features, measured by the detector:
lepton_pT: transverse momentum of the lepton
lepton_eta: pseudorapidity of the lepton (angle relative to the beam axis)
lepton_phi: azimuthal angle of the lepton (angle around the beam axis)
missing_energy_magnitude: magnitude of the missing transverse energy
missing_energy_phi: azimuthal angle of the missing transverse energy
jet1pt, jet1eta, jet1phi: transverse momentum, pseudorapidity and azimuthal angle of jet 1
jet1b-tag: b-tagging score of jet 1 (whether the jet probably comes from a b quark)
jet2pt, jet2eta, jet2phi, jet2b-tag: the same for jet 2
jet3pt, jet3eta, jet3phi, jet3b-tag: the same for jet 3
jet4pt, jet4eta, jet4phi, jet4b-tag: the same for jet 4

7 high-level features, invariant masses computed by physicists from the low-level features:
m_jj: invariant mass of two jets
m_jjj: invariant mass of three jets
m_lv: invariant mass of the lepton and the neutrino
m_jlv: invariant mass of a jet, the lepton and the neutrino
m_bb: invariant mass of two b-tagged jets
m_wbb: invariant mass of a W boson and two b-tagged jets
m_wwbb: invariant mass of two W bosons and two b-tagged jets

class: 1 - signal (Higgs bosons produced), 0 - background
