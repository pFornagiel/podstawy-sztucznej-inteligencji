Hotel Booking Demand (Resort Hotel)

Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jessemostipak/hotel-booking-demand
TidyTuesday mirror: https://github.com/rfordatascience/tidytuesday/tree/main/data/2020/2020-02-11
Original paper: N. Antonio, A. de Almeida, L. Nunes, "Hotel booking demand datasets", Data in Brief 22 (2019),
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2018.11.126
License: CC BY 4.0

Bookings of a resort hotel in the Algarve region (Portugal), with arrival dates between July 2015 and August 2017.
Personal information about customers was removed by the authors. This file contains only the resort hotel
("H1" in the original paper, "Resort Hotel" in the Kaggle version), the column "hotel" was removed.
40060 rows, 31 columns. The target variable is is_canceled.

Columns:

is_canceled: whether the booking was canceled (1) or not (0)
lead_time: number of days between the booking date and the arrival date
arrival_date_year: year of the arrival date
arrival_date_month: month of the arrival date
arrival_date_week_number: week number of the arrival date
arrival_date_day_of_month: day of the month of the arrival date
stays_in_weekend_nights: number of weekend nights (Saturday or Sunday) booked
stays_in_week_nights: number of week nights (Monday to Friday) booked
adults: number of adults
children: number of children
babies: number of babies
meal: type of meal booked
    BB - bed & breakfast
    HB - half board (breakfast and one other meal, usually dinner)
    FB - full board (breakfast, lunch and dinner)
    SC, Undefined - no meal package
country: country of origin, in ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 format
market_segment: market segment
    TA - travel agents
    TO - tour operators
distribution_channel: booking distribution channel
    TA - travel agents
    TO - tour operators
    GDS - global distribution system
is_repeated_guest: whether the booking was made by a repeated guest (1) or not (0)
previous_cancellations: number of previous bookings canceled by the customer, prior to the current booking
previous_bookings_not_canceled: number of previous bookings not canceled by the customer, prior to the current booking
reserved_room_type: code of the room type reserved (anonymized)
assigned_room_type: code of the room type assigned to the booking; it can differ from the reserved one, e.g.
    due to hotel operation reasons (overbooking) or customer request
booking_changes: number of changes made to the booking, from the moment it was entered into the system until
    the check-in or cancellation
deposit_type: whether the customer made a deposit to guarantee the booking
    No Deposit - no deposit was made
    Non Refund - a deposit was made in the value of the total stay cost
    Refundable - a deposit was made with a value under the total cost of stay
agent: ID of the travel agency that made the booking
company: ID of the company that made the booking or is responsible for paying
days_in_waiting_list: number of days the booking was on the waiting list, before it was confirmed to the customer
customer_type: type of booking
    Contract - booking with an allotment or other type of contract associated to it
    Group - booking associated to a group
    Transient - booking not part of a group or contract, and not associated to another transient booking
    Transient-Party - transient booking associated to at least one other transient booking
adr: average daily rate, i.e. sum of all lodging transactions divided by the total number of staying nights (in EUR)
required_car_parking_spaces: number of car parking spaces required by the customer
total_of_special_requests: number of special requests made by the customer (e.g. twin bed or high floor)
reservation_status: last reservation status
    Canceled - booking was canceled by the customer
    Check-Out - customer has checked in and already departed
    No-Show - customer did not check in and did not inform the hotel of the reason why
reservation_status_date: date at which the last reservation status was set (YYYY-MM-DD)
